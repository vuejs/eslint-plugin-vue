Added support for [ESLint Stylistic](https://eslint.style/) v6, see [its release notes](https://github.com/eslint-stylistic/eslint-stylistic/releases/tag/v6.0.0). Since ESLint Stylistic v6 changed the default option of `dot-location` to `"property"`, `vue/dot-location` also defaults to `"property"` when ESLint Stylistic v6 is installed.
